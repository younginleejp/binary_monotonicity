Gordon (2019) — taste shocks

With taste shocks the policy is a probability. Only the choices with P(i′|i) above machine epsilon have to be evaluated, and the same two properties locate that band. The two-period example of the paper, U(i,i′) = log(bi − bi′/2) + log(bi′).

  • 3-iBinary monotonicity with taste shocks n = 100, σ = 0.01 and 0.0001, as in Figure 1 of the paper
